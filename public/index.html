THE SHOP

More Than a Barbershop

Welcome to Dr. Beard, Allen Park's top-rated destination for expert grooming. Located on Ecorse Rd, our modern shop combines clean design with a welcoming atmosphere — honeycomb LED lights, sports memorabilia, and a vibe that keeps people coming back.

Ali and the team specialize in every hair type and texture. Whether it's a precision fade, a detailed beard sculpt, or your kid's first real haircut — you're in expert hands. Walk-ins always welcome, 7 days a week.
